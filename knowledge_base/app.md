# Getting the Most Out of the Mobile App

The mobile app is the fastest way to manage your account: check your bill or balance, track data usage, manage AutoPay or auto-recharge, and reach support without waiting on hold.

To get started, download the app from your device's app store, sign in with the credentials from your order confirmation, and confirm your line. Most account actions that are available on the website are also available in the app, plus a few app-only features like push notifications for low balance or bill-ready alerts.

If you're having trouble signing in, make sure you're using the same email or phone number associated with your account, and check that your line has finished activating first — the app can't fully load account details for a line that hasn't completed activation.

Downloading and signing into the app is optional but recommended: it's the quickest way to stay on top of your account during your first 30 days, and it's usually where the fastest resolutions to setup or billing questions happen.
