# eSIM vs. Physical SIM Setup

An eSIM is a digital SIM built into your device — there's no physical card to insert. A physical SIM is a removable card that ships with new-device orders or can be picked up at a store.

To set up an eSIM, open the activation email or text you received, scan the provided QR code (or tap the direct install link on the same device), and follow the on-screen prompts to download the eSIM profile. This usually takes under two minutes on a stable Wi-Fi or cellular connection.

To set up a physical SIM, power off your device, insert the SIM into the tray using the included tool or a paperclip, and power the device back on. The network should register automatically.

Most modern phones support eSIM, but not all — check your device's specifications if you're unsure. You can typically have both an eSIM and a physical SIM active at once (dual SIM) if your device and plan support it. If an eSIM QR code fails to scan, make sure you're using the device's camera app or Settings > Cellular > Add eSIM flow rather than a third-party scanner, since some third-party apps don't hand off eSIM profiles correctly.
