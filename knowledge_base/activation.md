# Activating Your New Line

When your order ships or your eSIM is ready, activation connects your device to the network so calls, texts, and data start working.

For a physical SIM: insert the card, power on your device, and wait up to a few minutes for the network to register. For eSIM, use the QR code or activation link from your order confirmation to install the profile, then toggle it on in your device's cellular settings.

If activation is still pending after 15 minutes, restart your device — most activation delays resolve with a simple restart because it forces the device to re-register with the network. If activation fails outright, double-check that the device is unlocked and compatible with the network, since a locked or incompatible device is the most common cause of activation failure.

Activation must complete before number transfer, network validation, or account security setup can be considered finished, since all onboarding steps for a line depend on the device successfully joining the network first.
