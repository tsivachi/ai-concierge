# What to Expect on Your First Bill

Your first postpaid bill usually arrives around three weeks after activation and is often higher than your regular monthly bill. This is expected, not a mistake.

The most common reason is proration: carriers typically bill from your activation date through the end of the current cycle, plus the full upcoming cycle in advance — effectively covering more than 30 days in one bill. On top of that, any one-time charges (activation fee, first device installment) and taxes/fees are usually included on this first bill rather than spread out.

To avoid surprises, review the itemized charges on your first bill rather than just the total — it will typically show the prorated amount separately from the regular recurring charge, so you can see exactly why the total is higher this once. From your second bill onward, the amount should settle into your normal recurring charge (plus any usage-based add-ons).

If your first bill still looks wrong after reviewing the itemized breakdown, that's worth raising as a billing question — and if you believe a specific line item is incorrect, it can be escalated as a dispute.
