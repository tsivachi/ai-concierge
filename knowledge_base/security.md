# Account Security Setup

Setting up account security protects your line from unauthorized changes — like someone else porting your number out or making changes to your account without your permission.

The main step is confirming your identity the first time you sign in to your account (through the app or website), which establishes your account's authenticated session going forward. From there, it's worth setting an account PIN or passcode, since carriers and support agents will ask for it before making sensitive changes like a port-out or a plan change.

If you ever suspect unauthorized activity on your account — an unexpected port request, a login you don't recognize, or a change you didn't make — that's treated as a sensitive security request and should be escalated to a human agent immediately rather than handled through self-service, since resolving it may require identity verification beyond what self-service can do.

Account security is a required step for every new line, alongside device activation and network validation, before onboarding is considered complete.
