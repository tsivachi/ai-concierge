# Understanding Your Plan and Data Usage

Your plan defines how much data, talk, and text you get each cycle, and what happens when you use it all.

You can check current usage in the app or website at any time — it typically shows how much data you've used against your plan's allowance for the current cycle, updated close to real time. Most plans either slow down (rather than cut off) data after you hit your allowance, or offer the option to purchase an add-on for more high-speed data.

If your data seems to be running out faster than expected, common causes include background app updates, video streaming quality set higher than needed, or a device set to use cellular data for Wi-Fi-only tasks like large downloads. Checking per-app data usage in your device settings can help identify the cause.

Prepaid plans in particular are worth checking regularly since running out of data or minutes before your renewal date is common if usage isn't monitored — the plan/data usage view is the fastest way to stay ahead of that.
