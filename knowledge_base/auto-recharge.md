# Setting Up Auto-Recharge (Prepaid)

Auto-recharge automatically refills your prepaid balance from a saved payment method before your plan expires, so your service doesn't lapse.

To turn it on, add a payment method in the app or website and enable auto-recharge — you can usually choose whether it triggers on your renewal date or when your balance drops below a certain threshold. Once enabled, you don't need to manually add funds each cycle.

Without auto-recharge, it's your responsibility to add funds manually before your renewal date; if your balance reaches zero before you renew, your service can pause until you add funds again. This is the single most common cause of unexpected service interruption on prepaid lines, which is why it's one of the first things worth setting up after activation.

You can turn auto-recharge off at any time, and doing so doesn't affect your current balance — it just means future renewals will require a manual top-up.
