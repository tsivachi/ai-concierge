# Basic Network Troubleshooting

If you have no signal, dropped calls, or slow data, a few quick steps resolve most issues without needing to contact support.

First, restart your device — this is the single most effective fix because it forces a fresh registration with the nearest cell tower. Second, check airplane mode is off and that cellular data is enabled in your device settings. Third, if you recently moved to a new area, give the device a few minutes to register with local towers, especially indoors or in areas with weaker coverage.

If the device still shows "no service" after a restart, confirm the SIM or eSIM is properly installed/activated (see the activation and eSIM/SIM guides) — a network problem is sometimes actually an incomplete activation. For eSIM devices, toggling the cellular line off and back on in settings can also help.

If none of these steps resolve it and the issue persists for more than a few hours, or you consistently have no service in an area where you previously had signal, this may indicate a device compatibility or network coverage issue worth escalating for a closer look.
