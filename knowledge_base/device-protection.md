# Device Protection Plans

Device protection covers repair or replacement costs if your phone is lost, stolen, or damaged — useful if you'd rather pay a small predictable monthly fee than a large repair bill later.

You can typically decide whether to add protection any time within the first month after activation; after that window, enrollment may require a device inspection to confirm there's no pre-existing damage. There's usually no penalty for declining — it's entirely optional and doesn't affect your onboarding completion either way.

If you do enroll, coverage generally includes a deductible for claims (a smaller fee you pay per repair or replacement, well below the phone's full cost) and a limit on how many claims you can file per year. Read the specific plan details before enrolling, since coverage tiers and deductibles vary by device.

If you're unsure whether you need it, consider how you'd cover an unexpected repair or replacement out of pocket — protection tends to make the most financial sense for newer, more expensive devices.
