# Using Your Line Internationally

Traveling outside your home country usually requires either an international add-on or roaming to be enabled before you leave — most plans don't include it by default.

Before you travel, check whether your plan includes international roaming or requires a separate add-on, and confirm your destination country is covered. Enabling this ahead of time avoids arriving and discovering you have no signal or data.

While abroad, data speeds and included allowances are often different from your domestic plan, and voice/text rates can differ by country — reviewing the specifics for your destination before you leave helps avoid unexpected charges. Wi-Fi calling and messaging apps are a reliable fallback if you're on a Wi-Fi network but don't have cellular coverage.

If you return from travel and still see international charges you weren't expecting, that's worth reviewing on your bill and raising as a billing question if a charge looks incorrect.
