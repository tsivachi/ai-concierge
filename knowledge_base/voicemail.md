# Setting Up Voicemail

Voicemail lets callers leave a message when you don't answer. It's not enabled automatically — you'll need to set it up once after your line activates.

To configure it, dial your own number's voicemail access code (usually by long-pressing "1" on the dial pad) or open your device's Phone app and follow the voicemail setup prompt. You'll be asked to create a PIN and record a greeting.

If you skip setup, callers will typically hear a generic "this mailbox has not been set up" message instead of being able to leave you a message — so it's worth completing even if you don't expect to use voicemail often. You can change your greeting or PIN at any time by returning to the same setup flow.

Visual voicemail (where messages appear as a list you can tap through, rather than needing to call in) may require the carrier's app or a device setting to be enabled separately, depending on your device.
