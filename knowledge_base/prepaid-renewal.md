# Prepaid Plan Renewal

Your prepaid plan renews on a set date each cycle, deducting the plan cost from your balance to keep your service active.

You can check your renewal date, current balance, and data allowance at any time in the app or website — the renewal-readiness view shows all three together along with whether auto-recharge is enabled. If your balance is enough to cover the renewal cost when the date arrives, renewal happens automatically; if not, service can pause until you add funds.

To avoid an interruption, either enable auto-recharge (see the auto-recharge guide) or make sure to manually add funds before your renewal date. It's worth checking a few days ahead of renewal, especially if you're not on auto-recharge, since a lapsed prepaid line typically needs funds added before it reactivates.

If your renewal date or balance looks different than expected, double-check for any add-ons or promotions that may have changed your renewal cost, since those affect how much is deducted at renewal.
