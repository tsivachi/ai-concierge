# Setting Up AutoPay

AutoPay automatically charges your saved payment method each billing cycle, so you never have to remember to pay manually or risk a late fee.

To set it up, add a payment method in the app or website, then turn on AutoPay for your account. You'll typically see a confirmation showing which card or bank account will be charged and on what day of your cycle.

Many plans offer a small monthly discount for enrolling in AutoPay, and it's one of the fastest ways to keep your account in good standing during your first 30 days — accounts without AutoPay are more likely to see a missed-payment issue simply because a manual payment was forgotten.

You can turn AutoPay off or change the payment method at any time; changes typically apply starting with your next bill, not retroactively to a bill that's already been generated. If a charge fails (for example, an expired card), you'll usually get a notification and a short grace period to update your payment method before a late fee applies.
